forever, wrapped

your whole spotify history, not just this year, with your photos on every era.

1

your spotify history

on spotify.com: account, privacy settings, then ask for extended streaming history. spotify emails you a zip (it can take a few days). drop that zip here.

2

your photos (optional)

pick photos from the years you listened. we read the date stored in each photo to put it on the right era. up to 400.

3

your claude api key

claude tags your top 500 songs with moods and themes and names your eras. it runs on your own key, usually $1 to $3. get one at console.anthropic.com.

used only while your record is being made, never saved. your files are deleted after 7 days.
